# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in AgentLens a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, and publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards. They may remove comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.
